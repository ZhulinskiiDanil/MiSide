# MiSide Menu

Geometry Dash's own screens dressed as **MiSide**'s interface, with an animated Mita on the right. Every button still presses GD's own, so GD's features and other mods' hooks keep working; where a screen is replaced, a way back to GD's own is kept.

## What it changes

- **Loading screen**: the AIHASTO studio intro (skippable, can be turned off), MiSide's loading jokes and a thin progress bar
- **Main menu**: a column of text items on the left with a soft shine, an animated 3D Mita on the right (click her face a few times), quitting asked in the column
- **Official levels** as MiSide's save-loading screen, with a floppy disk showing the chosen level
- **Pause menu**: the level blurred behind, a column with resume, restart, practice, volumes and settings; quitting asked first
- **Level complete** as a card with GD's own numbers and buttons
- **Achievements** and **statistics** as screens of their own
- **Every settings window of GD**: sound, all pages of More Options, graphics, the account, soundtracks, how to play, support, the editor's options, a level's options, the profile settings, and GD's dialogs while they're open
- **Appearance**: the mod's own settings (language, logo, intro, parallax, vanilla menu) and the credits

Everything else (the editor, the icon kit, online browsers, other mods' windows) is GD's as it was.

## Platforms

- **Windows** and **Android**: everything, with the real-time 3D Mita. On Android GRAPHICS and KEYS aren't there, as in GD on phones

## Controls

Mouse, keyboard (arrows, Enter, Esc, Left/Right for sliders) and a controller (A / B).

## Settings

- **Vanilla menu**: GD's own menu, exactly as without the mod
- **Menu language**: Russian or English
- **Studio intro**, **Logo**, **Parallax strength**, **Confirm leaving a level**
